# NAIJA360
## Product Requirements Document (PRD)

**Product:** NAIJA360  
**Product category:** AI-powered immersive tourism, culture, entertainment and social exploration platform  
**Document status:** Initial Product Requirements  
**Working tagline:** Nigeria is closer than you think.  
**Platform:** Web-first, responsive, designed for future immersive and multiplayer expansion.

---

# 1. Product Overview

## 1.1 Product Vision

NAIJA360 is an AI-powered immersive digital world that allows people anywhere in the world to explore, experience, discover and interact with Nigeria virtually.

The platform brings together Nigerian tourism, culture, heritage, entertainment, lifestyle, artificial intelligence, gamification and social interaction in one interactive environment.

Users should be able to explore Nigerian cities, walk through markets, visit restaurants, attend festivals, discover natural attractions, participate in recreational activities, interact with AI-powered Nigerian characters and meet other people exploring the country.

The experience should feel like entering a living virtual Nigerian world rather than visiting a conventional tourism website.

**The ultimate vision is to make Nigeria explorable, enjoyable and accessible to the entire world.**

## 1.2 Problem Statement

Many people interested in experiencing Nigeria face barriers such as:

- Geographical distance.
- Travel costs.
- Limited access to authentic Nigerian cultural experiences.
- Limited discovery of lesser-known destinations.
- Lack of engaging digital tourism experiences.
- Fragmented information about Nigerian destinations and heritage.
- Difficulty experiencing Nigerian culture interactively.

Existing tourism websites primarily provide descriptions, photographs, reviews and booking information.

NAIJA360 aims to go further by allowing people to experience Nigeria through interactive virtual environments, AI-guided discovery and multiplayer exploration.

## 1.3 Target Audience

1. Nigerians interested in discovering their own country.
2. Nigerians living abroad who want to reconnect with Nigerian culture.
3. International tourists interested in Nigeria.
4. Students and researchers interested in Nigerian history and culture.
5. Young people interested in entertainment, gaming and social experiences.
6. People who want to explore Nigeria but cannot physically travel.

## 1.4 Core Value Proposition

NAIJA360 combines:

- The discovery capabilities of a travel platform.
- The immersion of a virtual world.
- The entertainment of a game.
- The intelligence of an AI companion.
- The social experience of multiplayer exploration.
- The cultural value of an authentic Nigerian heritage platform.

Education should be integrated naturally into enjoyable experiences rather than becoming the entire purpose of the platform.

# 2. Product Objectives

The platform should:

1. Make Nigerian destinations accessible virtually.
2. Provide immersive and interactive experiences.
3. Promote authentic Nigerian culture and heritage.
4. Make exploration entertaining and rewarding.
5. Allow users to explore with friends and meet other users.
6. Provide intelligent, personalised recommendations.
7. Preserve and communicate credible Nigerian cultural information.
8. Support different Nigerian and international languages.
9. Create a scalable foundation for nationwide expansion.
10. Encourage repeat engagement through missions, rewards and new experiences.

## 2.1 Non-Goals for the Initial Release

The following should not be treated as mandatory launch requirements:

- Complete coverage of every Nigerian destination.
- Fully simulated 3D versions of all Nigerian cities.
- A fully autonomous AI video-generation engine.
- Community-submitted content without verification.
- The Nigerian Digital Passport.
- Advanced VR headset support.
- A complete simulation of real-world Nigerian infrastructure.

These can be developed as the platform evolves.

# 3. Core User Journey

## 3.1 Registration and Onboarding

The expected onboarding flow:

1. User opens NAIJA360.
2. User creates an account or logs in.
3. User creates and customises an avatar.
4. User chooses appearance, clothing and accessories.
5. User completes a personal profile.
6. User selects preferred language and interests.
7. User receives starter coins.
8. User enters the interactive Nigerian world.

Avatar customisation should include Nigerian-inspired clothing, accessories, traditional attire and contemporary Nigerian fashion.

## 3.2 Main Exploration Screen

The main destination after onboarding is an interactive map of Nigeria.

The map should be the primary navigation interface, not a secondary feature.

Users should be able to:

- Select a Nigerian state.
- Explore available cities and locations.
- Discover destinations and attractions.
- Browse experiences by category.
- View available activities and events.
- Discover recommended experiences.
- Ask AI where to start.
- Choose their own destinations.

The map should have a cinematic introduction showing Nigeria and allowing users to zoom into locations.

The experience should be visually engaging, interactive and easy to navigate.

## 3.3 AI-Assisted Discovery

Users who do not know where to begin should be able to ask the AI for recommendations.

Example:

"I want to experience something exciting in Nigeria."

The AI should recommend suitable experiences based on the user's interests, preferences and previous exploration.

Recommendations should link to actual available experiences.

Users must always retain the freedom to explore independently.

# 4. Experience and Content System

## 4.1 Geographic Exploration

The long-term geographic structure must support:

- All 36 Nigerian states.
- The Federal Capital Territory.
- Cities and neighbourhoods.
- Tourist destinations.
- Cultural landmarks.
- Natural attractions.
- Entertainment locations.
- Local communities.

The initial release should launch with a manageable selection of deeply developed experiences.

The architecture must support adding more locations without requiring a complete system redesign.

## 4.2 Experience-Based Navigation

Users must also be able to discover content through categories.

Initial category structure:

| Category | Example experiences |
|---|---|
| Wildlife and nature | Safaris, conservation centres, mountains and waterfalls |
| Culture and heritage | Traditional customs, folklore, kingdoms and indigenous communities |
| Festivals | Eyo Festival, Durbar and Osun-Osogbo Festival |
| Food | Nigerian dishes, restaurants, street food and food markets |
| Entertainment | Arcades, amusement parks, cinemas and concerts |
| Adventure | Hiking, outdoor activities and boat rides |
| Relaxation | Beaches, resorts and natural springs |
| Everyday Nigerian life | Markets, bukas, danfos, weddings and naming ceremonies |
| History | Museums, monuments and historical locations |
| Nightlife | Music, social venues and entertainment experiences |

Users should be able to explore by state or by category, with both systems connected.

## 4.3 Example Destinations

Examples for the content catalogue include:

- Lekki Conservation Centre.
- Yankari Game Reserve.
- Obudu Mountain Resort.
- Ikogosi Warm and Cold Springs.
- Nike Centre for Art and Culture.
- Badagry Slave Museum.
- Kalakuta Museum.
- Osun-Osogbo Sacred Grove.
- Ilorin Grand Durbar.
- Tarkwa Bay.
- La Campagne Tropicana.

These are proposed content examples, not a confirmed list of initial launch destinations.

## 4.4 Immersive Experience Formats

NAIJA360 should support three complementary formats.

### Interactive 3D environments

Users can:
- Move around.
- Walk through environments.
- Look in different directions.
- Enter buildings where supported.
- Interact with objects and characters.
- Encounter other users.

### Real-world 360-degree experiences

Users can explore authentic panoramic footage and recordings.

### Cinematic experiences

Users can watch guided videos and storytelling experiences.

The platform should select the most appropriate format for each experience.

The interface must clearly distinguish between:
- Authentic real-world media.
- Interactive 3D environments.
- AI-generated reconstructions.
- Cinematic storytelling.

Do not present generated reconstructions as authentic recordings.

# 5. AI System

## 5.1 AI Experience Generator

The AI should allow users to describe what they want to experience.

Example:

"I want to experience the Eyo Festival."

The system should:
1. Understand the request.
2. Identify the relevant destination or cultural experience.
3. Retrieve verified information.
4. Select suitable available media and interactive content.
5. Build a guided experience.
6. Provide narration and contextual information.
7. Allow users to ask questions during the experience.

The AI should assemble experiences from available, verified content rather than pretending to generate a fully interactive world from a text prompt when that capability does not exist.

## 5.2 AI Characters

The platform should include conversational AI characters representing different roles, such as:

- Market vendors.
- Food vendors.
- Cultural historians.
- Traditional storytellers.
- Local tour guides.

Characters should have:
- Distinct personalities.
- Regional knowledge.
- Appropriate language and expressions.
- Voice and text interaction.
- Contextual knowledge of their environment.
- The ability to answer questions and recommend activities.

Characters must be respectful and culturally informed. They must not rely on ethnic stereotypes or caricatures.

## 5.3 AI Memory and Personalisation

The platform should remember:

- Favourite destinations.
- Cultural interests.
- Preferred language.
- Completed experiences.
- Exploration history.
- Achievements.
- Previous conversations with AI characters.

This information should improve recommendations and allow users to resume previous experiences.

Users must be able to control their personal information and memory settings.

# 6. Cultural Intelligence and Content Authenticity

Accuracy is a critical product requirement.

The AI must retrieve information from credible sources rather than inventing cultural information.

## 6.1 Trusted Sources

Potential source categories include:

- Museums and cultural institutions.
- Government tourism and heritage resources.
- Historical archives.
- Academic publications.
- Credible Nigerian cultural organisations.
- Documented oral histories.
- Reputable documentaries and historical records.

## 6.2 Information Requirements

The system must:

- Attribute information to sources.
- Distinguish established historical facts from oral traditions.
- Acknowledge conflicting accounts.
- Avoid inventing traditions, customs and rituals.
- Flag uncertainty.
- Clearly label generated reconstructions.
- Avoid presenting unverified claims as facts.

Use controlled source retrieval and indexing. Do not implement unrestricted web scraping as the sole source of cultural knowledge.

## 6.3 Future Community Contributions

Future versions may allow verified Nigerians, historians, cultural practitioners, storytellers and local experts to submit:

- Historical accounts.
- Oral histories.
- Authentic photographs.
- Videos.
- Music and recordings.
- Local knowledge.
- Cultural explanations.

Submitted content must undergo verification before publication.

# 7. Audio and Atmosphere

Environmental sound should contribute to immersion.

Examples:

- Market bargaining.
- Danfo conductors.
- Traffic.
- Street conversations.
- Nigerian music.
- Festival drums and chants.
- Crowd reactions.
- Wildlife sounds.
- Ocean and nature ambience.

AI characters should have spoken voices.

Spatial audio should be supported where the environment and technology allow it.

The platform must respect audio licensing and cultural rights.

# 8. Social and Multiplayer System

NAIJA360 should function as a shared virtual world.

## 8.1 Avatar Encounters

Users should encounter other users naturally inside virtual environments.

Users should be able to:
- See nearby avatars.
- Approach other users.
- Initiate conversations.
- Start exploring together.
- Invite friends into existing sessions.

## 8.2 Communication

The initial interaction priority is text chat.

Voice chat should be an additional option.

Users may:
- Communicate through text.
- Start optional voice conversations.
- Participate in group conversations.
- Mute or leave conversations.
- Manage interaction permissions.

## 8.3 Group Exploration

Users should be able to:

- Invite friends.
- Join group exploration sessions.
- Explore locations together.
- Attend virtual festivals.
- Participate in cultural competitions.
- Share discoveries and achievements.

The technical architecture should support synchronised multiplayer sessions.

## 8.4 Safety

The platform should include appropriate safeguards:

- Report and block users.
- Mute other users.
- Control incoming invitations.
- Manage privacy settings.
- Moderate inappropriate content.
- Prevent harassment and impersonation.

These safeguards are important because users interact with strangers in shared spaces.

# 10. Gamification and Coin Economy

## 10.1 Starter Rewards

New users receive starter coins when joining.

## 10.2 Earning Coins

Users can earn coins through:

- Daily login rewards.
- Login streaks.
- Completing missions.
- Completing experiences.
- Cultural quizzes.
- Discovering new locations.
- Participating in activities.

## 10.3 Spending Coins

Users can spend earned coins to unlock:

- Special experiences.
- Premium experiences.
- Additional content.

Users may also purchase additional coins if they want to access more content.

## 10.4 Wallet

The wallet should track:

- Available balance.
- Coins earned.
- Coins spent.
- Rewards.
- Transactions.

Coin balances must be maintained securely on the backend.

Coin purchases and spending must use server-side validation. Never trust client-submitted coin balances.

The platform should offer meaningful free exploration and avoid making the entire experience pay-to-access.

# 14. Non-Functional Requirements

## Performance
- Optimise initial page loading.
- Lazy-load large media assets.
- Use responsive image and video delivery.
- Avoid loading entire 3D environments unnecessarily.
- Provide graceful loading and error states.

## Security
- Secure authentication.
- Server-side authorisation.
- Input validation.
- Rate limiting.
- Secure API endpoints.
- Protection against unauthorised wallet modifications.
- Safe handling of user-generated content.

## Scalability
- Support additional states, destinations and experiences.
- Allow new AI characters and languages.
- Support future multiplayer growth.
- Keep content data separate from presentation logic.

## Accessibility
- Responsive desktop and mobile experiences.
- Keyboard-accessible controls.
- Captions and text alternatives.
- Text-based communication as an alternative to voice.
- Accessible contrast and typography.

## Reliability
- Handle unavailable media gracefully.
- Handle failed AI responses.
- Prevent duplicate coin transactions.
- Preserve user progress.
- Provide useful error messages.

# 17. Acceptance Criteria

The initial product should not be considered complete until:

1. A user can register and log in.
2. A user can create and save an avatar.
3. A user can enter the main exploration screen.
4. The Nigeria map provides functional navigation.
5. Users can explore by state and category.
6. Users can open available destination experiences.
7. Users can interact with the AI discovery system.
8. AI responses use available verified content and do not fabricate sources.
9. Users can interact with supported AI characters.
10. Users can earn and spend coins through validated backend operations.
11. User profiles and exploration progress persist.
12. The application works across desktop and mobile screen sizes.
13. Unavailable features are clearly identified rather than represented as functional.
14. Errors, empty states and loading states are handled properly.
15. The project builds successfully and its core tests pass.

**Final product principle:** NAIJA360 should feel like entering Nigeria, not opening another tourism website.
